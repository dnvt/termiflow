# Visual lesson: complex TD title portals need source-owned straight lanes

Status: Promoted local rule; broader sibling portal ownership remains a P2 watch
Source epoch: P2 subgraph-complex TD title/portal corridor, 2026-08-25
Fixture family: `subgraph_complex_td`

## Observation

In the complex two-subgraph TD scene, an external API entry and a cross-layer
edge previously formed compact title-adjacent elbows at the Service and Data
boundaries. The routes were connected, but the tiny corners could read as a
damaged title hook or a border junction. The corrected frame keeps the API and
cross-layer target-facing shafts straight through their declared portal cells;
all six arrows, both titled groups, database shapes, labels, and the final
response fan-in remain visible.

## Hypothesis and falsifier

The exact flat TD/TB topology can safely use a source-owned target lane when
the selector proves two titled sibling groups, distinct receiver nodes,
unlabeled acyclic edges, and sufficient clearance. The route lowerer and fanout
projection must share that lane, while mixed, nested, labeled, cyclic, or
ambiguous scenes fail closed.

The hypothesis is falsified if any ASCII or Unicode, default or optimized,
canonical or authored-policy homolog loses an arrow, overwrites a title or
border, introduces a corner directly beside an arrowhead, detaches a portal,
or visually merges two target entries. `collision_parallel_edges_bt` and the
other sibling/corridor fixtures remain negative controls; this rule must not be
exported to them without a new visual cycle.

## Evidence

- Focused regression: `tests/default_print.rs` test
  `subgraph_complex_td_portal_corridor_has_no_mini_hook_artifact`; run with
  `cargo test --locked --all-features --test default_print
  subgraph_complex_td_portal_corridor_has_no_mini_hook_artifact -- --exact`.
- Complete-corpus result: 242 inputs, 968 packet rows, 956 renderable rows,
  and 12 separately governed expected-error rows in each canonical and
  authored/no-override policy lane.
- Human-eye result: every renderable row has a hash-bound perceptual decision;
  the four changed rows in each lane were inspected directly and the former
  title/portal hooks were not visible. Existing topology-ambiguous watches
  remain recorded for unrelated families.
- Golden result: snapshot approval remains a separate intent-bound gate after
  the clean-source packet and dependency checks.

Transient packets, private Maestro capsules, model prompts, and local absolute
paths are intentionally omitted from this public lesson.

## Promoted rule or next experiment

Promote only this exact selector and its shared lane contract. The next
experiment is a topology-owned corridor/portal-ownership improvement for the
remaining long sibling rails and fallback routes, with a fresh complete corpus
review in both policy lanes before any generalization or golden approval.
